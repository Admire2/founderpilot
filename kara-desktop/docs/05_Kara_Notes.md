# 📓 Kara Notes: Sovereign Second Brain & Knowledge Base

## Chapter 1: Introduction & Core Purpose
Kara Notes is your personal, offline wiki and knowledge management system. It replaces tools like Notion or Obsidian, giving you a private space to capture ideas, meeting minutes, and research without cloud dependency.

## Chapter 2: Key Features & Functionalities
- **Template Engine**: One-click creation of Meeting Minutes, Project Kickoffs, Daily Standups, and Research briefs.
- **Bi-Directional Wiki-Links**: Type \[[Note Name]]\ to create instant, clickable connections between related notes.
- **Local AI Summarization**: Extract action items and summaries from messy notes using your local Ollama instance.
- **Promote to Document**: Instantly convert a raw note into a formatted Kara Word document.

## Chapter 3: Step-by-Step Usage Guide
1. **Create**: Click "New Note" and select a template, or start blank.
2. **Link**: Type \[[Project Alpha]]\ and switch to "Preview Links" mode to see it become a clickable navigation link.
3. **Summarize**: Click "AI Summarize" to let the local AI read your note and generate a clean, bulleted action list below it.
4. **Export**: Click "Promote to Word" to open the note in Kara Word for final polishing and PDF export.

## Chapter 4: Intersections & Ecosystem Integration
- **To Kara Word**: The primary exit door. Notes are raw; Word is polished. "Promote to Word" bridges this gap seamlessly.
- **To Kara Intelligence**: Click "Publish" to send the note to the Intelligence bus, making it available for Combined Reports with data from Kara Base or Sheets.
