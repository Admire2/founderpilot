# 🧠 Kara Intelligence: The Sovereign AI Routing Bus

## Chapter 1: Introduction & Core Purpose
Kara Intelligence is not just an app; it is the central nervous system of the entire Kara Office suite. It collects "artifacts" (data, notes, summaries) from all other apps and allows you to route, merge, and transform them using local AI.

## Chapter 2: Key Features & Functionalities
- **Artifact Collection**: A centralized drawer that lists every item published from Word, Sheets, Base, Notes, etc.
- **Multi-Format Routing**: One-click buttons to send any artifact "To Word" (as a document) or "To Slides" (as a deck).
- **Combined Report (AI)**: Select multiple artifacts, and the local AI will read them all and write a cohesive, multi-source executive summary.
- **Local Download**: Export any artifact as a raw .txt file for external use.

## Chapter 3: Step-by-Step Usage Guide
1. **Publish**: In any app (e.g., Kara Notes), click "Publish" or "AI Brief".
2. **Open Drawer**: Click the "Kara Intelligence" button in the top navigation bar.
3. **Route**: Find your artifact. Click "To Word" to open it in the word processor, or "To Slides" to turn it into a presentation.
4. **Combine**: Check the boxes next to 2 or more artifacts. Click "Combined Report (AI)".
5. **Review**: The AI will process the data and open a new, merged document in Kara Word.

## Chapter 4: Intersections & Ecosystem Integration
- **The Ultimate Connector**: Kara Intelligence is the reason Kara Office is an "ecosystem" and not just isolated tools. It ensures that a database in Base, a note in Notes, and a grid in Sheets can all be fused into a single, board-ready PDF report in seconds, entirely offline.
