# 📅 Kara Calendar: Sovereign Scheduling & Time Management

## Chapter 1: Introduction & Core Purpose
Kara Calendar is a local-first scheduling tool. It replaces cloud-dependent calendars (like Google Calendar) with a private, offline grid for managing your time, meetings, and deadlines.

## Chapter 2: Key Features & Functionalities
- **Monthly Grid View**: A clean, intuitive calendar interface with navigation controls.
- **Event Management**: Add, view, and delete events with specific titles and times.
- **Mail-to-Calendar Bridge**: A dedicated integration that allows you to schedule emails instantly.
- **JSON Import/Export**: Back up your schedule or migrate it to other devices.

## Chapter 3: Step-by-Step Usage Guide
1. **Open**: Select "Kara Calendar" from the sidebar.
2. **Navigate**: Use the arrows to change months, or click "Today" to jump to the current date.
3. **Add Event**: Click on any day in the grid. Type a title and time in the right panel, then click "Add".
4. **Manage**: View your daily schedule in the right panel. Click the Trash icon to remove an event.
5. **Export**: Click the Download icon to save your events as a .json backup.

## Chapter 4: Intersections & Ecosystem Integration
- **From Kara Mail**: The most powerful feature. Open any email, click "To Calendar", and the email's subject is instantly pre-filled as a new event in Kara Calendar for today.
- **From Kara Connect**: Future automations will allow Kara Base deadlines to auto-populate as Kara Calendar events.
