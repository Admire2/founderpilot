The financial truth behind their empires

  • Amazon sellers average $290K/yr in sales but keep only 20-30% after costs.
  • eBay monetizes ~14% of its $79.6B GMV; Etsy squeezes ~24.5% consolidated; Shopify takes ~3% of GMV but charges rent regardless of sales.
  • Every giant gets paid whether the seller eats or not. KaraKata gets paid only when the seller eats.
