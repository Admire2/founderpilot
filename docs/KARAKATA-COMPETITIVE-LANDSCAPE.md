# KaraKata Competitive Landscape — The Amazon+Shopify Hybrid, Measured
*FounderPilot AI · Marketplace Suite · September 10, 2026*

**The positioning in one line:** KaraKata = **Amazon's demand engine** (buyers come to you) + **Shopify's seller empowerment** (every seller gets a real storefront with real analytics) + **what neither has**: emerging-market rails and an AI suite baked into every listing.

---

| Player | Model | Headline analytics | Scale | Growth | The gap KaraKata attacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amazon** | Marketplace king | GMV >$830B in 2025; 3P sellers = $575B; 3P = 61–62% of paid units; total revenue $716.9B, 3P services $172.2B | ~1.9M active 3P sellers | Mature | Fees + ads squeeze margins; artisans buried; no custom-commission flow |

| **eBay** | Legacy auction/listing | FY25 GMV $79.6B (+7%); revenue $11.1B; 136M active buyers | ~18M sellers | +7% | Aging buyer base; listing fatigue |
| **Temu** | Fully-managed price war | ~$100B GMV 2025; 133.6M US monthly actives | Huge, opaque | Blazing | Sellers get no brand, no pricing power, no analytics |

## 🎯 The hybrid decoded

- **From Amazon we take:** curated demand, trust layers, concierge-verified quotes, fulfillment discipline.
- **From Shopify we take:** every seller gets a branded storefront + analytics + tools — listing copy from our Landing Page tool, pricing from our Pricing tool, QR codes, Pia receipts — **the suite IS the seller dashboard**.
- **What only KaraKata has:** emerging-market rails (Jumia's playbook, AI-speed), an AI suite baked into every listing, and an affordability ledger that lets every person, sector, and factor participate at near-zero cost.

## ✅ The war map

Amazon owns scale. Shopify owns tooling. Temu owns price. TikTok owns attention. **Nobody owns AI-empowered general commerce for emerging markets at near-zero seller cost.** That's KaraKata's beachhead.
