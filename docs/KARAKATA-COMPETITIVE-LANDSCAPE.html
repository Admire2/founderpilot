🎯 The hybrid decoded

  • From Amazon we take: curated demand, trust layers, fulfillment discipline.
  • From Shopify we take: every seller gets a branded storefront + analytics + tools — listing copy from our Landing Page tool, pricing from our Pricing tool, QR codes — the suite IS the seller dashboard.
  • What only KaraKata has: emerging-market rails (Jumia's playbook, AI-speed), an AI suite baked into every listing, and an affordability ledger that lets every person, sector, and factor participate at near-zero cost.

✅ The war map

Amazon owns scale. Shopify owns tooling. Temu owns price. TikTok owns attention. Nobody owns AI-empowered general commerce for emerging markets at near-zero seller cost. That's KaraKata's beachhead.
