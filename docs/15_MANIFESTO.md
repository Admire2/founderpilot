# THE KARA OFFICE MANIFESTO

**Your work is yours. Your words, your numbers, your designs, your mail — none of it should rent a home in someone else's cloud.**

Kara Office is a complete, AI-native productivity suite that runs 100% on your machine:
Word, Sheets, Slides, Mail, Notes, Base, Calendar, Flow, Connect, Design, Drive, Sync — united by one Intelligence bus and one local AI brain.

- **No subscriptions.** Buy once, own forever.
- **No telemetry.** Zero bytes leave your device unless you export them yourself.
- **No internet required.** Works in a bunker, on a plane, in a blackout.
- **No vendor lock-in.** Every artifact exports to open formats: .docx, .csv, .pdf, .png, .json, .md.

We built Kara Office because sovereignty is not a feature. It is the foundation.
