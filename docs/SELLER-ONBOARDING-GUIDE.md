## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Onboarding

### Step 1: Merchant Registration
- Go to [KaraKata Seller Center](https://founderpilot-theta.vercel.app/karakata/sellercenter)
- Sign up using your business email.
- **Crucial:** Select "Phase 1 Founding Merchant" during registration to lock in lower lifetime fees.

### Step 2: Identity & Business Verification (KYB)
- To maintain our high-trust ecosystem, we require verification.
- Upload your Business Registration Certificate (or Government ID for sole traders).
- *Note: Verification typically takes 24-48 hours.*

### Step 3: Build Your Storefront
- **Store Name:** Choose a name that reflects your brand identity.
- **Banner & Logo:** Upload high-resolution assets.
- **About Us:** Tell your story. Buyers connect with the *origin* of the product.

### Step 4: List Your First Product
- **Title:** Be descriptive (e.g., "Hand-Woven Aso-Oke Executive Blazer" instead of just "Jacket").
- **Category:** Select the most accurate category to ensure visibility.
- **Pricing:** Set your price in USD. Our system handles multi-currency conversion for global buyers.
- **Inventory:** Accurate stock counts prevent order cancellations.
