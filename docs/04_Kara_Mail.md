# 📬 Kara Mail: Sovereign Email Composition & Management

## Chapter 1: Introduction & Core Purpose
Kara Mail is a local-first email client interface designed for secure drafting, AI-assisted replies, and approval workflows. It ensures your sensitive communications never train third-party AI models.

## Chapter 2: Key Features & Functionalities
- **AI Draft Assistant**: Generate professional email bodies from simple prompts (e.g., "Follow up on the Georgia Tech partnership").
- **AI Reply Assistant**: Read an incoming email and instruct the local AI to draft a context-aware response.
- **Approval Workflow**: A "Review Before Sending" modal ensures no AI-generated hallucinations are sent accidentally.
- **Sovereign Storage**: Drafts and sent items are stored locally in your browser.

## Chapter 3: Step-by-Step Usage Guide
1. **Compose**: Click "Compose" in the sidebar. Fill in To, Subject, and use the purple "AI Draft Assistant" box to generate the body.
2. **Review**: Click "Send (Review First)" to open the approval modal. Verify the text.
3. **Reply**: Open an email from the Inbox. Use the green "AI Reply Assistant" box to type your intent (e.g., "Accept meeting for Thursday 2pm") and click "Suggest Reply".
4. **Send**: Approve the generated reply and send via your default mail client (mailto: link).

## Chapter 4: Intersections & Ecosystem Integration
- **To Kara Calendar**: Open any email and click the "To Calendar" button. This instantly creates a scheduled event in Kara Calendar with the email subject as the title.
- **To Kara Notes**: Copy email text and paste it into Kara Notes, or use Kara Intelligence to summarize long email threads into actionable notes.
