# 🗄️ Kara Base: Sovereign Relational Database

## Chapter 1: Introduction & Core Purpose
Kara Base is a local-first, Airtable/Notion-database alternative. It allows you to structure unstructured data into relational tables with typed columns, all stored securely on your device.

## Chapter 2: Key Features & Functionalities
- **Typed Columns**: Support for Text, Number, Select (Dropdown), and Checkbox data types.
- **Dual Views**: Toggle instantly between a Spreadsheet-style **Grid** view and a **Kanban** board view.
- **One-Click Sample Data**: Instantly load a "Dangote Rollout Plan" to test functionality.
- **AI Briefing**: Local AI reads your entire database and generates an executive summary of totals, risks, and statuses.

## Chapter 3: Step-by-Step Usage Guide
1. **Create**: Click "New Base" in the left sidebar.
2. **Structure**: Click "Add Column", name it, and select its type (e.g., "Status" as Select).
3. **Populate**: Click "Add Row" and fill in the data. Or, click "Sample Data" to load a pre-built project plan.
4. **Visualize**: Click the "Kanban" button at the top to group your rows by the "Status" column.
5. **Export**: Click "Export CSV" or "Export JSON" to back up your database.

## Chapter 4: Intersections & Ecosystem Integration
- **To Kara Flow**: Tasks identified in Kara Base can be manually or (in future V2.1) automatically pushed to Kara Flow for sprint tracking.
- **To Kara Intelligence**: Click "Publish" or "AI Brief" to send the database state to the Intelligence drawer for high-level reporting.
