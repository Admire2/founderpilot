# 📝 Kara Word: Sovereign Word Processor

## Chapter 1: Introduction & Core Purpose
Kara Word is the flagship document creation engine of the Kara Office suite. It is a 100% local, offline-first, privacy-respecting alternative to Microsoft Word. It requires no internet connection, no subscriptions, and sends zero telemetry to third parties.

## Chapter 2: Key Features & Functionalities
- **Rich Text Editing**: Full support for bold, italic, lists, headings, and alignment.
- **AI Ghost Completion**: Local AI (via Ollama) suggests text as you type, keeping your workflow uninterrupted.
- **Grammar & Spell Check**: Real-time, local validation of your text without sending data to the cloud.
- **Sovereign Storage**: Documents are saved directly to your browser's local storage or exported as standard .docx/.pdf files.

## Chapter 3: Step-by-Step Usage Guide
1. **Open**: Click "Kara Word" in the left sidebar.
2. **Create**: Click "New Document" or "Open" to load a saved file.
3. **Edit**: Start typing. Use the top toolbar to format text.
4. **AI Assist**: Press the designated hotkey or click the AI button to let Qwen 2.5 suggest the next sentence.
5. **Save/Export**: Click "Export" to download your work as a .docx or .pdf file.

## Chapter 4: Intersections & Ecosystem Integration
- **From Kara Notes**: Use the "Promote to Word" button in Kara Notes to instantly convert a brainstorming session into a formal, formatted Word document.
- **From Kara Intelligence**: Open the Intelligence drawer, select any artifact (e.g., a Base summary), and click "To Word" to generate a professional report instantly.
