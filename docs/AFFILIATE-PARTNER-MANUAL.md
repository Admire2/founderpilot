# KaraKata Affiliate Partner Manual
### Strategy: Carpeting Africa, Conquering the World

**Confidential Internal Document**

## 🌍 The Mission
We are not just recruiting sellers; we are building the **Infrastructure Plug of Africa**. Your role as an Affiliate Partner is to identify high-value producers, artisans, and manufacturers who are currently underserved by global giants and bring them into the KaraKata sovereign ecosystem.

---

## 💎 The Commission Structure

### 1. Founding Ambassador Tier (Invite Only)
- **Commission:** **5% Lifetime** on all sales generated by your referred sellers.
- **Requirement:** Must onboard 10 verified, high-quality sellers within the first 60 days.
- **Status:** Permanent. Even if you stop active recruiting, you earn 5% on your network's volume forever.

### 2. Standard Partner Tier
- **Commission:** **2.5% Lifetime** on all sales generated by your referred sellers.
- **Requirement:** Open to all approved affiliates.

---

## 🧠 The "Credit Multiplier" Strategy
*How to close more deals and earn more:*

When pitching to sellers, encourage them to opt-in to the **Credit Payout Model** (receiving 20% of earnings in Kara Credits).
- **Why?** Sellers who hold credits are more sticky (they stay on the platform) and have higher purchasing power within the ecosystem.
- **Your Bonus:** Affiliates receive a **one-time bonus** for every seller who activates the Credit Payout Model.

---

## 🛡️ Compliance & The 60-Day Rule
To protect the ecosystem from fraud and "churn-and-burn" tactics:
- **The Clearing Period:** Commissions are calculated immediately but enter a "Pending" state for 60 days.
- **Why?** This covers the window for returns, disputes, and chargebacks.
- **Release:** After 60 days, funds move to "Available Balance" for withdrawal.

---

## 🎯 Who to Target (The "Ideal Seller Profile")
1. **The Artisan:** High-skill craftsmen (leather, textiles, jewelry) who lack global distribution.
2. **The Agro-Processor:** Farmers who have moved beyond raw crops to packaged goods (shea butter, spices, coffee).
3. **The Tech-Maker:** Local hardware or accessory manufacturers.
