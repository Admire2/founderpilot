# 📜 The KaraKata Doctrine
*Founder-locked · September 10, 2026 · FounderPilot AI — Marketplace Suite*

## 1 · KaraKata is a name, not a niche
KaraKata does what Amazon and Shopify do — connects buyers and sellers at scale — but **AI-empowered**, so the whole machine runs at a fraction of the cost. It is a general commerce platform, not a vertical experiment.

## 2 · Build-It-For-Me goes to the back burner
The photo-to-build RFQ flow stays in the drawer as a later feature. It is not the headline, not the hook, not the first build. It switches on only after liquidity exists.

## 3 · The real product = minimal resources at all three ends, at AI speed
- **Our end (platform):** no ops army — AI categorizes, moderates, triages support, flags fraud; humans touch exceptions only.
- **Seller end:** no photographer, no copywriter, no designer, no $39/mo — one phone photo + rough words → a live, priced, discoverable listing.
- **Buyer end:** no search fatigue — ask in plain language, get a shortlist with price benchmarks, buy in taps.

## 4 · Affordability is the design constraint, not a marketing line
Every person, sector, and factor in the loop must be able to participate at near-zero cost. Fees exist only where value was delivered.
