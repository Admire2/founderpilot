# 🚀 Kara Office: Quick Start Guide

Welcome to **Kara Office**, your 100% sovereign, offline-first, AI-native productivity suite. No cloud, no subscriptions, no data harvesting.

## 🧭 1. Navigation
- **Left Sidebar**: Your command center. Click any app icon to switch contexts instantly.
- **Dashboard**: Your home base. Provides a high-level overview of your suite.
- **Kara Intelligence (Top Bar)**: The brain of the suite. Click the "Intelligence" button to open the side drawer. This is where artifacts from all apps are collected, combined, and routed.

## 🧠 2. The Golden Rule: "Publish & Route"
Kara Office apps talk to each other seamlessly via the **Intelligence Bus**.
1. In **Kara Notes**, write a brief and click **Publish** (or **AI Brief** in Kara Base).
2. Open the **Intelligence Drawer** (top bar).
3. Select your artifact and click **To Word**, **To Slides**, or **Combined Report (AI)** to instantly generate a formal document.

## 💾 3. Your Data is Yours (Import/Export)
- Everything is saved automatically to your device's local storage.
- **Export**: Use the Export buttons in Sheets (`.csv`), Base (`.json`/`.csv`), Slides (`.pdf`/`.json`), and Design (`.png`) to create local backups.
- **Import**: Bring your existing data in. Sheets and Base accept `.csv` files; Slides accepts `.pdf` and `.docx` to auto-generate decks.
- **Kara Sync**: Use the Sync app to export a full "Bundle" (`.json`) of your entire workspace to move safely to another computer.
