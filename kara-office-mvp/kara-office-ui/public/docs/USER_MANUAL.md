# 📖 Kara Office: Comprehensive User Manual

## 📝 Kara Word
- **Purpose**: Professional document creation.
- **How to Use**: Type naturally. Use the top toolbar for formatting.
- **AI Features**: "Ghost Completion" suggests text as you type. "Check Grammar" highlights errors.
- **Integration**: Open documents directly from Kara Notes via "Promote to Word".

## 📊 Kara Sheets
- **Purpose**: Sovereign spreadsheet and data analysis.
- **How to Use**: Click cells to edit. Use the top bar to add rows/columns.
- **Quant Mode**: Built-in financial formulas (Black-Scholes, Greeks) for advanced modeling.
- **Import/Export**: Click "Import CSV" to load external data. Click "Export CSV" to save your grid.

## 📽️ Kara Slides
- **Purpose**: Presentation deck builder.
- **How to Use**: Click "+" to add slides. Use the right panel to edit Title and Body text.
- **AI Deck Builder**: Type a topic in the AI prompt to generate a full deck instantly.
- **Import**: Click "Import" to turn a `.pdf` or `.docx` file into a slide deck (one slide per page/section).
- **Export**: Click "Export PDF" to generate a board-ready presentation file.

## 📬 Kara Mail
- **Purpose**: Sovereign email composition and management.
- **How to Use**: Click "Compose" to write. Use the Inbox to read.
- **AI Draft**: In Compose, type a prompt (e.g., "Follow up on the Dangote proposal") and click Generate. The AI writes the email body for you.
- **AI Reply**: Open an email, type a prompt in the green "AI Reply Assistant" box (e.g., "Accept and propose Thursday 2pm"), and it will open a pre-filled reply.
- **To Calendar**: Open any email and click "To Calendar" to instantly schedule it.

## 📓 Kara Notes
- **Purpose**: Your sovereign "Second Brain" and knowledge base.
- **How to Use**: Click "New Note" or choose a Template (Meeting Minutes, Kickoff, etc.).
- **Wiki-Links**: Type `[[Note Name]]` and switch to "Preview Links" mode to create clickable connections between notes.
- **AI Summarize**: Click the button to have local AI extract action items and summaries.
- **Promote to Word**: Turns your note into a formatted Word document instantly.

## 🗄️ Kara Base
- **Purpose**: Relational database (Airtable/Notion alternative).
- **How to Use**: Create a Base, add Columns (Text, Number, Select, Checkbox), and add Rows.
- **Views**: Toggle between **Grid** (spreadsheet view) and **Kanban** (card view grouped by Select columns).
- **AI Brief**: Click to have local AI read your database and write an executive summary.

## 🎨 Kara Design
- **Purpose**: Sovereign vector graphics and layout tool.
- **How to Use**: Select a tool (Rectangle, Circle, Text) and click "Add to Canvas".
- **AI Design Assistant**: Type a prompt (e.g., "A red circle with white text saying Hello") and click Generate.
- **Edit**: Drag shapes to move them. Drag the bottom-right white dot to resize. Use the color picker to change colors.
- **Templates**: Click "Social", "Banner", or "Logo" for instant starting layouts.
- **Export**: Click "Export as PNG" to download your design.

## 📦 Kara Drive
- **Purpose**: Client-side encrypted file vault.
- **How to Use**: Click "Upload to Vault". The file is encrypted locally before being stored.
- **Download**: Click the Unlock (🔓) icon to decrypt and download the file to your computer.

## ⚡ Kara Flow
- **Purpose**: Project and task management.
- **How to Use**: Add a task with a title, assignee, and due date.
- **Kanban**: Drag and drop tasks between "To Do", "In Progress", and "Done" columns.

## 🔌 Kara Connect
- **Purpose**: Local workflow automation (Zapier alternative).
- **How to Use**: Create rules like "When Kara Mail receives email → Kara Calendar creates event". (V1 is a visual rule builder; V2 will execute them).

## 🔄 Kara Sync
- **Purpose**: Offline workspace backup and transfer.
- **How to Use**: Click "Export Sync Bundle" to download a `.json` file of your entire Kara Office state. Click "Import Bundle" on another machine to restore it.

## 📅 Kara Calendar
- **Purpose**: Local scheduling and time management.
- **How to Use**: Click a day on the grid, type an event title and time, and click "Add".
- **Mail Bridge**: Use the "To Calendar" button in Kara Mail to auto-schedule emails.
